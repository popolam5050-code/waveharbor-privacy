Privacy policy

WaveHarbor for Android · com.hushquay.waveharbor
Effective date: 13 October 2026

WaveHarbor is a podcast player developed by HushQuay Workshop. You can listen without creating an account. Account sign-in and cloud library synchronization are optional. Contact the developer responsible for the processing described here at hushquayworkshop@gmail.com.

1. Data on your device

The app stores your subscriptions, favorites, card order, listening history and progress, playback queue, bookmarks and notes, settings, downloaded episodes and cached artwork on your device. These support playback, offline listening and your library. Audio files and artwork caches are not included in WaveHarbor cloud library synchronization. Android backup or device transfer may separately handle eligible local app data according to your device settings.

2. Optional account and cloud synchronization

If you register using email and password or sign in with Google, Google Firebase Authentication processes your account email, user identifier, authentication credentials and, where supplied by Google, basic profile information such as your name and profile image. Firebase also processes technical connection information, such as IP addresses and user agents, for authentication and abuse prevention. The developer does not receive your Google password. Email/password credentials are handled by Firebase Authentication.

After you confirm connecting your library, the app stores an account-linked copy in Google Cloud Firestore: subscribed feed URLs and subscription changes; favorites and card order; episode identifiers, playback positions, completion status and listening timestamps; bookmark titles, notes, timestamps and associated episode metadata; and the manual queue with episode titles, audio/artwork URLs and related metadata. Modification timestamps and deletion markers allow changes to merge. Sync runs manually and approximately once a day when Android allows it and a network is available. Signing out stops scheduled synchronization but does not delete the existing cloud copy.

3. Reports you choose to send

When you submit a problem report, we receive its category, description, reproduction steps, optional contact email, app version and build date, manufacturer and model of the device, Android version, language, a report identifier and submission timestamp. Reports use a separate Firebase anonymous user identifier, including when you are not signed in to the library account. That identifier and the last report timestamp help prevent spam. Reports are not posted publicly. Links in report text are removed by the app; do not include passwords, private feed credentials or other secrets. Reports do not automatically attach recordings, screenshots or your full listening library.

4. Podcast catalogs, publishers and external links

Search queries are sent to the catalogs used by the app: Apple Podcasts/iTunes, fyyd and gPodder. Loading feeds, artwork, streaming and downloads connects your device directly to podcast publishers, their hosting services and content delivery networks. Those services receive your IP address, requested resource and technical request information, and may use their own measurement or advertising systems. Background feed checks and enabled automatic downloads also make these connections.

Opening links in episode descriptions, Instagram, PayPal or Boosty takes you to an external service governed by its own privacy policy. Donations are processed externally; WaveHarbor does not collect card or bank details. The app does not add advertising SDKs, Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. Podcasts themselves may contain advertising.

5. Why we process data

We use local data to provide the features you select, account and sync data to provide the optional cloud service, and reports to investigate problems and respond if you provide an email. Where the GDPR applies, processing necessary to deliver a requested service relies on performance of that service; essential security and support processing relies on legitimate interests in operating a reliable app and preventing abuse. Where consent is required, you may withdraw it for future processing. We do not sell your personal data or use your listening library for targeted advertising.

6. Service providers and security

Google Firebase provides authentication and cloud storage. Google processes service data under its applicable terms; see Firebase privacy and security information and Google Privacy Policy. Support email is handled through Gmail. The policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process technical request logs; see GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cloud connections use encrypted transport. Firestore rules restrict library access to the authenticated owner and restrict access to support reports. The developer has administrative access needed to operate the service. This is not end-to-end encrypted storage. Third-party RSS or media URLs may use HTTP if provided that way by a publisher. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Providers may process data outside your country, including the United States. Their applicable data-processing terms and transfer safeguards govern those transfers. We do not promise that all data stays in Germany or the European Union.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it, clear app storage or uninstall, subject to Android backup/transfer behavior. Account and synchronized library records are retained until you request their deletion. Support reports and related anti-abuse records are kept while needed to investigate the reported issue, handle follow-up or prevent abuse, and are removed when no longer needed; there is no automatic fixed expiry configured. You may request deletion sooner. Minimal records may be retained where necessary to meet legal obligations or address security abuse, and any applicable exception will be explained to you.

Deleting the app or signing out does not delete your cloud account. To request deletion of the Firebase account and its synchronized library, use Delete account & data or email us. Reports use a separate anonymous identity: include a report reference or the contact email used in the report if you also want reports removed. Removing an account does not erase copies already present on other devices or data independently held by podcast providers. Provider backups and technical logs follow the provider's retention rules; Firebase describes removal of authentication data from live and backup systems within 180 days after the developer initiates account deletion.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use the player without a cloud account, sign out to stop sync, control background checks and automatic downloads in settings, change notification permissions through Android, and clear local app data. To request access, correction, export, deletion or restriction of personal data, or object to processing, contact hushquayworkshop@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable verification of account ownership, but never your password. Where applicable, you can complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children and policy updates

WaveHarbor is a general podcast player and is not specifically directed to children. Podcast content is supplied by independent publishers. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, contact us. We will update this page when practices change and revise the effective date. Material changes will be communicated through the app or other appropriate means.
