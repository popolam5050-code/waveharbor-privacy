You can request deletion without reinstalling WaveHarbor.

Request deletion by email

If the button does not open your mail app, write directly to hushquayworkshop@gmail.com.

What to send

  1. Use the subject “WaveHarbor account deletion”.
  2. Send the request from the email associated with your WaveHarbor email or Google sign-in account, if possible. Identify the account email in your message.
  3. Say whether you want the entire account deleted, only the cloud library deleted, or a support report deleted. For a report, include its reference or the email you supplied in it, if available.

Never send your password, Google sign-in codes, card details or identity documents in your initial request. We may ask for proportionate confirmation of ownership before deleting account data.

What we delete

For account deletion, we delete the Firebase Authentication account and its Firestore cloud library, including subscriptions, favorites, ordering, listening progress/history, bookmarks/notes and manual queue. Separately identifiable support reports and associated rate-limit records can also be removed on request. Because reports use a separate anonymous identifier, we may need the report reference to locate them.

Requests are handled manually, without undue delay; where GDPR deadlines apply, normally within one month. If an extension or lawful retention exception applies, we will explain it. Service-provider backup/log retention may continue after deletion from the active service; Firebase describes authentication data removal from live and backup systems within 180 days after deletion is initiated.

Local copies and other devices

Sign out of WaveHarbor on all devices before deletion to stop further synchronization. Clear app storage or uninstall if you also want to remove the local library and downloaded episodes. Account deletion does not remotely erase local copies or independent publisher data. Creating an account again or connecting another account later can upload a library still stored on the device.

The same request page is available from Settings → About in the app version that includes the privacy links. Read the privacy policy.
